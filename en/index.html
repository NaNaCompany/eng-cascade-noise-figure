Enter values and select Calculate.

FORMULA

F_total = F₁ + Σᵢ₌₂ⁿ (Fᵢ−1)/(G₁…Gᵢ₋₁); NF = 10 log₁₀F_total

Assumptions

Add stages in order from input to output. Both power gain and noise figure are entered in dB; 0 dB gain means unity and negative gain means attenuation. Required: a common frequency and 290 K noise reference, matched interstage connections and uncorrelated added noise. Noise figures are nonnegative. Excluded: impedance mismatch, reverse coupling, oscillation, compression and differing mixer sideband conventions.

Displayed values are floating-point approximations. Design safety factors and unprovided error contributions are not calculated.
